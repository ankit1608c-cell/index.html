🏍️ Bike Meter Tracker

Take a meter photo, confirm the reading, and save it with location.

Digital displays and rotating number wheels are both supported, but OCR can misread digits. Keep the meter straight, well-lit, and in focus. Always check the number before saving.

Use the live camera to line up either a digital display or rotating number wheels. Tap Scan live frame to read the digits from what the camera currently sees. The captured frame becomes the saved evidence photo.
If live camera access is unavailable, you can still upload/take a photo below.
Location will be requested when you take a photo.

How daily kilometres are calculated

Enter the morning reading when the employee arrives at the office and the evening reading separately after returning. Daily kilometres = evening reading − morning reading. The Google Sheet backend below stores each session as a separate row, including the photo link and location. It flags missing pairs or an evening reading lower than the morning reading for review.

Important setup

This page needs the Google Apps Script Web App URL pasted into the code below. OCR uses an online library, and camera/location permissions work best when this page is hosted on HTTPS (for example, GitHub Pages) rather than opened as a local file.
